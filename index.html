About Sharon

An architect who became a strategist.

I know both sides of the table.

I'm an architect who became a brand and narrative strategist, not a marketer who learned to speak architecture.

I've spent my career inside design practices, working alongside architects, engineers, and other technical experts. I understand the complexity of the work, the ambition behind it, and the very real difficulty of stepping outside it long enough to explain why it matters.

So we don't start by judging the story. We start by understanding the work.

Then we find what's worth saying, build the narrative around it, and make sure it survives contact with the real world.

The goal isn't to make your work sound like marketing. It's to make the value of the work impossible to miss.
