Privacy & Cookies

This page explains what data this site collects, what cookies it sets, and the choices you have.

What this site collects

The only data collection on this site is Google Analytics, and only after you accept the cookie banner. When active, it records standard analytics information: pages visited, general referral source (e.g. which search engine or site sent you here), device/browser type, and an approximate geographic region. Google Analytics does not store your full IP address.

Cookies

No cookies are set on your first visit. If you accept analytics via the cookie banner, Google Analytics sets its standard cookies (typically named _ga and _ga_*) to distinguish visitors across sessions. If you decline, no analytics cookies are set at all. This site does not use advertising, retargeting, or social-media tracking cookies of any kind.

Third-party services

Google Analytics (operated by Google LLC) is the only third-party service embedded on this site. No other trackers, chat widgets, embeds, or social pixels are present.

Your choices

  • Decline the cookie banner at any time to opt out of analytics entirely.
  • Clear your browser's local storage for this site to reset your choice and see the banner again.
  • Use a browser extension or setting (e.g. "Do Not Track," ad blockers) to block Google Analytics regardless of this site's own setting.
